Kia ora, I'm Kelly Blincoe

I study how people create and maintain software systems. I am a Rutherford Discovery Fellow and a Professor of Software Engineering at the University of Auckland, New Zealand in the Human Aspects of Software Engineering Lab (HASEL), in the Department of Electrical, Computer, and Software Engineering. I lead the ENGclusion research programme.
